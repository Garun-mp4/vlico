## Итоговый промпт

Use case: professional architectural interior photo reconstruction based on a reference. Input image 1 is the scene reference/edit target, not a style board. Create ONE photorealistic premium architectural photograph of this same ceiling-and-light concept, reconstructed with correct perspective, immaculate professional photographic quality and careful editorial color grading. Output a high resolution portrait 3:4 composition, preferably 1536x2048 or higher native resolution. This will be a large portrait image in a dark luxury ceilings-and-lighting studio website, not a background for text. Preserve the reference's recognizable architecture: smooth matte greige stretch ceiling, concealed continuous LED perimeter line turning smoothly around the rounded plaster corner on the left, pale mineral plaster walls, floor-to-ceiling evenly spaced natural oak vertical slats on the right, the perpendicular back-ceiling junction. Recompose and straighten the camera perspective: perfectly vertical architectural uprights, level back-wall horizontals, optically corrected straight lines, no leaning walls, no fisheye, no Dutch angle. Use a professional tilt-shift architectural lens around 24–28mm, looking upward enough to make the ceiling and integrated lighting the subject, but retain enough walls for spatial context. The central ceiling and the whole important perimeter-light junction must be visible comfortably inside the frame; do not push critical LED corners against the image edges. A finished uncluttered interior, no open bathroom/toilet, no construction mess, no added furniture, people, signs, logos, watermarks or text. If the doorway is visible keep it a subtle closed flush interior door in the lower background. Lighting: finely controlled architectural photography with restrained natural fill, realistic continuous warm-neutral LED ~3000–3500 K, preserved LED detail without blown white clipping, no harsh bloom or haze, no artificial glowing wooden slats, clean shadows, tactile natural oak grain and realistic fine plaster texture. Matte ceiling must be perfectly smooth and clean, without stains, blotches, mottled compression or plastic shine. Color grade: refined warm neutral grays, natural muted oak, calibrated whites, clear but soft contrast, deep rich shadows with detail. Sharp optical detail throughout, precise geometry, high-end interiors magazine editorial quality, not a glossy CGI render, not oversharpened, no excessive HDR. The original low-resolution blur, noise, skew, green/yellow cast and JPEG artifacts must be gone. Produce the interior image only, with no webpage UI or composited screenshot.
